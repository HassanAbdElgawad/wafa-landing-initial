Privacy Policy - Wafa App

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Account information such as name, mobile number, and email address.
  • Location data to suggest nearby branches or brands (only after user consent, and can be disabled anytime from device settings).
  • Usage and interaction data with loyalty points or stamp programs.
  • Device information such as device type, operating system, and IP address.

How We Use Information

  • Improve our service and personalize the customer experience.
  • Send app-related notifications and updates.
  • Enhance service quality and analyze performance.
  • Comply with applicable regulations and legal requirements.

Information Sharing

We may share data with:

  • Authorized government entities when legally required.
  • Trusted partners under agreement and privacy obligations.
  • We do not sell or rent your data to any third party.

Customer Rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), customers may:

  • Request access to their personal data.
  • Request correction or updates of their data.
  • Request deletion of data within applicable legal limits.
  • Exercise these rights by contacting our support channels.
